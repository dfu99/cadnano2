# Tone and Style Guide

## Voice
- Direct, objective, scientific.
- First person plural ("we") is acceptable for describing our own work.
- No hedging, no filler, no rhetorical flourish.

## Structure
- Each section: statement, supporting evidence, conclusion.
- Transitions between sections should be logical, not clever.
- Section titles are descriptive and plain. No wordplay, no parenthetical asides.

## Formatting
- No emdashes. Use commas, semicolons, colons, or separate sentences.
- No italic "lesson" subheadings (e.g., "What we learned:").
- Captions are factual. No editorializing.
- 2x12, 2x14, etc. are "cross-sectional dimensions", not "widths".

## Citations
- Cite where our observations agree with existing literature.
- Do not overclaim. The agent is not the designer; the human provides the domain knowledge.

## What to Avoid
- AI-slop patterns: "What we learned", "The key insight", "Why this matters" as standalone headings.
- Quirky or informal phrasing in titles or captions.
- Emdashes anywhere in the document.
